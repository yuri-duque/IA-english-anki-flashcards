# quite

- **Word:** quite
- **Meaning:** <b>bastante</b>; bem; muito; completamente (em certos contextos)
- **PartOfSpeech:** adverb
- **Definition:** To a noticeable or considerable degree; in some contexts, completely.
- **Example:** The test was <b>quite</b> difficult, but everyone passed.
- **ExampleTranslation:** A prova foi bastante difícil, mas todos passaram.

# quite
*What does this word mean and how is it used?*

## Verso
<b>bastante</b>; bem; muito; completamente (em certos contextos)

_adverb_

> To a noticeable or considerable degree; in some contexts, completely.

### How to use
Use <b>quite</b> before an adjective or adverb to mean “bastante” or “bem.” With some adjectives expressing an absolute or complete state, it can mean “completamente,” as in <b>quite impossible</b>. Its strength depends on context and can differ between British and American English.

### Example
The test was <b>quite</b> difficult, but everyone passed.

_A prova foi bastante difícil, mas todos passaram._

### Common expressions
<b>quite good</b> — bem bom; bastante bom; <b>quite a lot</b> — bastante; muito; <b>quite sure</b> — bastante seguro; completamente certo
